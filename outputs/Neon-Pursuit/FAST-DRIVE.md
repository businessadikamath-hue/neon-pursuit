Strong acceleration through 180-200 mph, a taper toward each game's speed limit, and gentle coasting. These are authored game measurements, not real-car performance. Flat-road results advance the actual automatic powertrain at 120 Hz. The integrated road includes hills and curves, with Lane Assist on and geography penalties and traffic off.
